
* PARTY GAMES *

Neverball features a variety of "Party Game" modes inspired by classic arcade titles. These modes can be played solo or with up to 4 players (some modes support AI opponents).

To access these modes, select "Party Games" from the main menu.

* MODE LIST

1. MONKEY TARGET
   Fly your ball off a ramp and land on a target in the ocean.
   - Controls:
     - Tilt to steer.
     - Press Action (A) to open/close wings.
     - Pitch Up (Stick Down) to gain altitude (loses speed).
     - Pitch Down (Stick Up) to dive (gains speed).
   - Goal: Land in the center (Bullseye) for 500 points.

2. MONKEY BATTLE (SHARED)
   A race to the finish line on a single shared screen.
   - Features:
     - All players share one camera. If you fall behind, you warp to the leader.
     - Items: Missile (Stun leader), Banana (Drop hazard).

3. MONKEY FIGHT
   Knock opponents off the platform.
   - Controls:
     - Press Action (A) to extend your boxing glove.
   - Features:
     - Hitting an opponent applies a massive impulse.
     - Last monkey remaining wins.

4. MONKEY BILLIARDS
   Standard 8-ball style pool (simplified).
   - Controls:
     - Hold Action (A) to charge power.
     - Release to shoot the cue ball.
   - Goal: Sink all colored balls. Don't sink the white ball!

5. MONKEY BOWLING
   Ten-pin bowling.
   - Phases:
     1. Position: Move Left/Right. Press A to lock.
     2. Aim: An arrow sweeps L/R. Press A to lock.
     3. Power: A bar fills up. Press A to lock.
   - Goal: Knock down as many pins as possible.

6. MONKEY GOLF
   Minigolf mechanics on standard levels.
   - Controls:
     - Rotate camera to aim.
     - Hold Action (A) to charge power.
     - Release to putt.
   - Features:
     - Stroke counter is displayed.
     - High friction "grass" physics.

7. MONKEY BOAT
   Row a boat down a river course.
   - Controls:
     - Tap Left (Tilt Left) to paddle left.
     - Tap Right (Tilt Right) to paddle right.
     - Alternate L/R to build speed.
     - Hold both to brake.

8. MONKEY SHOT
   On-rails FPS shooter.
   - Controls:
     - Stick moves the cursor.
     - Action (A) fires.
     - B Button reloads.
   - Goal: Shoot targets and enemies.

9. MONKEY SOCCER
   Giant soccer match!
   - Teams:
     - Red Team (Players 1 & 3) vs Blue Team (Players 2 & 4).
   - Gameplay:
     - A giant soccer ball spawns in the center.
     - Push the ball into the opponent's goal (Z+ or Z-).
     - First to score wins the round.

10. MONKEY TENNIS
   1v1 or 2v2 Tennis Match!
   - Controls:
     - Use Action (A) to swing your racket.
     - Hit the ball over the net.
     - Standard scoring applies.

11. MONKEY BASEBALL
   Home Run Derby!
   - Controls:
     - Pitcher: Aim with Stick, Throw with A.
     - Batter: Time your swing (A) to hit the ball.
     - Score points for distance!

12. WHACK-A-MOLE
   Whack the Moles!
   - Gameplay:
     - A grid of moles will pop up and down continuously.
     - Roll over the moles while they are "up" to score points.
     - You will be bounced up slightly upon a successful whack.
     - The player with the most points when the timer runs out wins!

13. HAMMER THROW
   Olympic Monkey Hammer!
   - Controls:
     - Spin the stick or tilt left/right rapidly to build up throw speed.
     - The charge meter will show your current rotational power.
     - Press Action (A) to release the ball.
   - Gameplay:
     - The ball will be thrown forward with an arc based on your speed.
     - Points are awarded equal to the distance traveled before coming to rest.

14. MONKEY DOGFIGHT
   Aerial Combat!
   - Gameplay:
     - Your monkey is permanently in flight mode (glider deployed).
     - Steer your monkey through the sky.
     - Press Action (A) to fire your machine gun at opponents in front of you.
     - Each hit deals 10 damage. A player is destroyed (and respawns) at 100 damage.
     - Destroying a target awards you 50 points.
