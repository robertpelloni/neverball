
                            * PARTY GAMES *


Neverball includes a suite of Party Games designed for multiplayer fun.
These modes can be played with 1-4 players in split-screen mode.

To access these games, select "Party Games" from the main menu.


* MONKEY TARGET

Fly your monkey ball through the air and land on the target to score points!

Controls:
    Tilt Control:   Steer the ball during the roll phase.
    Action Button:  Open wings to glide (at the end of the ramp).
                    Close wings to drop/land.
    Up/Down (Air):  Control pitch. Pull Up to slow down/gain altitude.
                    Push Down to speed up/lose altitude.

Scoring:
    Red Zone (Bullseye):  500 Points
    Yellow Zone (Inner):  300 Points
    Blue Zone (Outer):    100 Points
    White Zone (Edge):     50 Points

Tips:
    - Build up speed on the ramp before opening your wings.
    - Use the Wheel of Danger items to disrupt opponents!
    - Land carefully! If you are moving too fast, you will roll off.
    - Brake by pulling up just before landing.


* MONKEY FIGHT

Knock your opponents off the stage to be the last monkey standing!

Controls:
    Tilt Control:   Move your monkey ball.
    Action Button:  Punch! (Extends a boxing glove).

Power-ups:
    Speed:          Temporary speed boost.
    Big Punch:      Increases punch range and knockback.


* MONKEY BILLIARDS

A simplified billiards game. Sink all the balls to win!

Controls:
    Tilt Control:   Aim your shot.
    Action Button:  Hold to charge power, release to shoot.

Rules:
    - Sink balls to earn points (100 pts each).
    - Don't sink the cue ball (Scratch)!


* MONKEY BOWLING

Ten-pin bowling with a monkey ball twist.

Controls:
    Step 1: Position. Use Left/Right to position your monkey.
            Press Action to confirm.
    Step 2: Aim. An arrow will sweep back and forth.
            Press Action to stop it at the desired angle.
    Step 3: Power. A power meter will fill up.
            Press Action to lock in the power and throw!

Scoring:
    Standard bowling scoring applies (Strikes, Spares).


* BATTLE MODE (RACE)

Race against your friends on any existing Neverball level!

Controls:
    Standard Neverball controls apply.

Power-ups:
    Speed:          Boosts your speed.
    Missile:        Homing missile that stuns the leader.
    Banana:         Slip hazard.
