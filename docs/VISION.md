# Neverball Project Vision

## The Ultimate Goal
The ultimate vision for Neverball is to evolve it from a classic open-source marble roller into a **definitive, feature-complete spiritual successor to the Super Monkey Ball (SMB) series**, while retaining its own unique identity and level design legacy.

This means achieving 100% feature parity with the entire SMB franchise (spanning SMB 1, 2, Deluxe, and modern titles like Banana Blitz and Banana Mania) and incorporating the best elements of *Marble Blast* (such as active jumps, spin dashes, and power-ups).

## Core Design Pillars

### 1. Gameplay & Physics Excellence
*   **The "Arcade Feel":** Provide an option for tight, high-friction, instantly responsive physics that mimics the arcade cabinets of the early 2000s, contrasting with Neverball's traditional "heavy" momentum.
*   **Modern Movement:** Integrate active abilities seamlessly. Every character should have access to an active Jump and a chargeable Spin Dash, fundamentally changing how levels can be traversed and speedrun.
*   **Character Diversity:** Implement a robust stat system (Weight, Speed, Acceleration, Jump Height) tied to a diverse cast of unlockable characters.

### 2. The Ultimate Party Game Engine
*   Neverball should be the premier open-source local multiplayer game.
*   The engine must flawlessly support 4-player split-screen.
*   **The Roster:** Implement every major minigame from the SMB lineage:
    *   *Tier 1 (Core):* Monkey Race, Monkey Target, Monkey Fight.
    *   *Tier 2 (Sports):* Monkey Bowling, Monkey Billiards, Monkey Golf, Monkey Tennis, Monkey Soccer, Monkey Baseball.
    *   *Tier 3 (Arcade):* Monkey Boat, Monkey Shot, Whack-a-Mole.

### 3. Campaign & Narrative
*   Transition from a simple linear level list to a rich, world-based structure.
*   Implement Hub Worlds with Warp Gates allowing non-linear progression.
*   Integrate story cutscenes (in-engine or video) between major worlds.

### 4. Progression & Economy
*   Every action in the game (completing levels, playing party games) should reward the player with persistent currency ("Bananas" or "Coins").
*   Implement an In-Game Shop where currency can be spent to unlock:
    *   New Characters and Costumes.
    *   New Party Game Modes.
    *   Concept Art and Soundtracks.

### 5. Robust Engineering & Documentation
*   The C codebase must remain clean, performant, and dependency-light.
*   **Zero Magic:** Every feature must be exhaustively documented in `manual.txt`, `party_games.txt`, and represented fully in the UI with tooltips, descriptions, and visual feedback.
*   **Extensibility:** The architecture should pave the way for future networked multiplayer and in-game level editing.

## The Promise
"Keep going. Don't ever stop." The project is committed to continuous, autonomous improvement until every facet of this vision is realized in software, fully polished, and bug-free.
